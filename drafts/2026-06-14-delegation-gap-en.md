---
78% of AI failures are invisible. Stanford analyzed 196K coding conversations: users accept wrong code, fake citations, missing requirements — never noticing. And 94% of these hidden failures persist even with smarter models. Better AI isn't the fix. Better interaction design is.
---
Counterintuitive stat: 27% of AI work creates NEW tasks. AI doesn't shrink your backlog — it expands it. Engineers build internal tools, fix papercuts they'd never prioritized before. The real bottleneck isn't writing code anymore. It's deciding what deserves to be built.
